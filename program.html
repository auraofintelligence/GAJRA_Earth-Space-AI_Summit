No date, no locked venue, no pretend schedule

A program shape for attracting the right first allies.

This page names the kinds of rooms a future gathering might need. It is a conversation starter for partners, not a final timetable.

Program principle

The first format should be a working commons.

The seed docs keep pointing to rooms where people can test the architecture, not just listen to speeches: yarning circles, source-review studios, civic-twin demos, open science sessions and practical support commitments.

This is why the page avoids a fake timetable. The useful move now is to identify the right rooms and the first serious allies for each room.

Room concepts

A summit that behaves like a working commons.

Opening circle: why Brisbane, why now

Set the invitation in place: Brisbane, Minjerribah, South East Queensland, Pacific relationships, Olympic runway energy and the need for trust before scale.

Minjerribah proof-of-care studio

Translate sand-to-stars thinking into public-safe local patterns: shared tables, training pathways, maker capacity, cultural protocol and practical resilience.

Care economy lab

Explore C-Hours, care receipts, co-operative structures, regenerative asset language and practical ways to recognise contribution without turning carers into a gig market.

Civic AI and sovereignty stack

Map how individual, neighbourhood, bioregional and planetary layers can share useful intelligence while protecting raw personal and cultural data through edge-first design.

Peaceful space commons roundtable

Bring space scientists, legal thinkers, defence-transition minds and open science builders into a cooperative pathway for peaceful orbital stewardship.

AUKUS Space Gambit and ISS preservation studio

Test the specific 2030-2031 fork: NASA's SpaceX deorbit pathway is the public baseline, while the seed-doc proposal asks whether Australia could convene a lawful, peaceful ISS preservation alternative before that decision becomes irreversible.

Open AUKUS Space Gambit

Eclipse readiness and open science studio

Use the 2028 and 2030 Australian eclipse markers to plan safe viewing, public sky literacy, visitor movement, communications drills and open science storytelling.

Web3 Sensorium and source review

Test how open astronomical data, public evidence trails, verifiable credentials and careful hypothesis labels could help science debate stay useful.

Health and AI evidence review

Separate public wellbeing themes from private clinical claims, then decide what needs formal review before any health material becomes outward-facing.

Public evidence and agent-readable notes

Turn big ideas into inspectable briefs, Markdown records, source trails and careful public pages that can be reviewed by humans and AI agents.

Supporter commitments room

End with grounded offers: a venue introduction, a hosted roundtable, research help, public communications, funding, legal review or a local pilot.

Still to decide

The missing pieces are part of the ask.

International cultural protocol

Any On Country, First Nations, Pacific or international knowledge layer needs proper relationship, consent, data sovereignty and trusted review before it becomes a public invitation.

BCEC and format pathway

Brisbane Convention & Exhibition Centre is the natural large-room candidate, but the format should scale from real support: briefing salon, partner roundtable, then conference only when the table is ready.

Hypothetical disclosure relevance

UAP, underwater-civilisation and celestial-hypothesis material belongs in a labelled hypothesis sandbox: useful for scenario planning, not presented as settled evidence.

Evidence and sci-fi permeability

The CU and Strange but True material can help people feel possible futures. The public page should name the boundary between evidence, speculative design and fiction so the bridge stays honest.

Narrative safety and trust

Security, espionage and AI threads need information hygiene: no implied real-world misconduct, no unreviewed partner claims, and no private or cultural material exposed for drama.
